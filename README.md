# Vidferry

A video downloader for macOS. Paste a link, or find the video playing in the built-in browser, choose a quality or extract only the audio, and download with multiple threads.

[简体中文](#简体中文)

## Download and install

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple Silicon. The interface is available in English, 简体中文, 日本語 and Tiếng Việt.

1. Download [Vidferry.zip](https://github.com/leadead/Vidferry-Releases/releases/latest/download/Vidferry.zip) ([all versions](https://github.com/leadead/Vidferry-Releases/releases))
2. Unzip it and drag Vidferry to Applications

The app is notarized by Apple.

## Updates

Vidferry checks for updates automatically. You can also choose Check for Updates… in the Vidferry menu.

## Privacy

Vidferry collects and uploads nothing. It connects only to the sites you open, the addresses needed to parse them, the update feed in this repository, and the advertising list (easylist.to) about once a week.

## Please note

Download only content you have the right to save. Vidferry does not support DRM-protected videos.

## Third-party components

Vidferry includes FFmpeg (LGPL 2.1 or later), yt-dlp (Unlicense), Deno (MIT), Sparkle (MIT) and EasyList (CC BY-SA 3.0). Versions, the FFmpeg source address and the full licenses are in the app at `Contents/Resources/ThirdPartyLicenses.txt`.
